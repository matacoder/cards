# Verb forms

Decisions (2026-11-01):

- One card per verb. The first show is the infinitive, then each show takes the next form, with the translation in the same form. The tick marks the whole verb as learned, so levels and counters stay as they are.
- Forms: present, preterite, imperfect, future, and the imperative. Rare moods are out.
- Spanish first: it is the language being learned. Translations into Russian and English.
- Forms come from a conjugation library, not from an LLM. An LLM only picks one meaning per verb (imperfective and perfective Russian verb, English verb); Russian and English forms are then inflected by libraries.
- Verbs that don't translate form by form (gustar, haber as auxiliary, impersonal verbs) stay infinitive only.
- Speech reads the form on screen, not the infinitive.
